Uzbek Palov Recipe

What You Need

  • 4 cups rice
  • Onions, carrots
  • Beef
  • Sunflower oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. First, fry the beef in sunflower oil until it turns brown.
  2. then add onions and carrots.
  3. Warning: use medium heat when cooking the beef.
  4. Wash the rice and drain it.
  5. then add the rice to the pot and cook for a few minutes.
